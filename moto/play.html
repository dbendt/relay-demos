Land with the bike matching the ground's slope: within 15° is clean (on a down slope, a little faster), up to 30° rough, beyond that a crash. The throttle warms the engine to 50 % at most; turbo heats it fast, and over 100 % the bike stops for 3 s. Coasting, the air and cool zones bring it down.
